A special birthday gift platformer adventure for twin sisters **Janani** and **Janika**!

### How to Install on Android
1. Download the `.apk` file onto your Android phone.
2. Open your phone's file manager or browser downloads and tap the APK.
3. If prompted, tap **Settings** and allow **Install unknown apps** for your browser or file manager.
4. Tap **Install** and enjoy playing!

## 🎮 The Story & Characters

* **Janani** — The brave hero and playable character traversing meadows, sky ruins, desert dunes, and shadowy graveyards.
* **Janika** — Her twin sister, awaiting rescue at the end of World 1.
* **Kitty** — A loyal feline companion who follows Janani, offers hints, and transforms into **Super Kitty** during boss encounters!
* **Yuva** — The mischievous, horn-wearing boss who challenges Janani. Stomp him 3 times to win!

## 🕹️ Controls

### On Mobile (Touch Screen)
* **Left Thumb:** Left and Right movement.
* **Right Thumb:** Diamond pad with Jump, Up (ladders & doors), Down (crouch & climb down), Sword Attack, and Magic Shoot (fireball/arrow when charged).
* **Back:** Opens Pause Menu or navigates back.

### On PC (Keyboard)
* **Arrow Keys / WASD:** Move, jump, climb ladders.
* **Space:** Jump.
* **J / Z / Attack:** Sword strike.
* **K / X / Shoot:** Fire magic (when charged).
* **Esc:** Pause / Back.

## 📁 Repository Structure

* `godot/` — Godot 4.7.2 project (GDScript, GL Compatibility renderer, multi-floor levels, cinematic lighting, touch pad).
* `unity/` — Unity 6.6 2D URP project.
* `game/` — Phaser 4 + Vite birthday backup.
* `tools/` — Asset generation, tile export, and automation scripts.
